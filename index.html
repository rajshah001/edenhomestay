our story

Tucked above the town,
deep in the valley

Eden’s Homestay sits on the quiet Chongay Tar hillside, just above the State Bank of India’s Chongay branch and beside the Padma Odzer Choeling School — a stone’s throw from the bustle of Gangtok, yet a world away. Recently renovated, every room opens to mountain and valley views.

Mornings begin with clouds lifting off the ridgelines. Days drift between the garden, the sun terrace and home-cooked Sikkimese meals. Evenings end around the outdoor fireplace. Come for a night — or, as our guests do, for a month-long detox from city life.

“Nestled deep within valleys, facing the snow-capped mountains and clear skies during winters, and warm, lush, fog-marked summers. Perfect for a one-day unwinding or a month-long detox from the hustles of busy city life.”— from Eden’s own listing

A taste of Sikkim

Local, organic, made to order — and breakfast is on the house. Sikkim has been India’s first fully organic state since 2016, and you’ll taste exactly why.

🥟

Steamed dumplings, the valley’s love language.

🍜

Thukpa
